- Use hyphens (-) instead of spaces
- Keep original words - DO NOT shorten or optimize names

  ✓ band-photo-promotional-shot.jpg

  ✗ EaglesDare.jpg (no capitals)
  ✗ eagles dare.jpg (no spaces)

Image Paths in Markdown:
- From this file: images/photos/live/your-photo-name.jpg
