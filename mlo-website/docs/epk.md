## About the Band

Named after William Gibson's cyberpunk masterpiece, **Mona Lisa Overdrive** creates immersive sonic experiences that blur the lines between the nostalgic and the futuristic, the analog and the digital.

Our sound draws from synthwave's retro-futuristic aesthetics, vaporwave's digital dreamscapes, and modern electronic production techniques. Each performance is designed to transport audiences into a neon-lit world where sound, light, and energy converge.

Based in Wilmington, NC, we're not just a band—we're building an entire world. Our approach to live performance goes beyond music, creating complete sensory experiences that engage audiences on multiple levels.
